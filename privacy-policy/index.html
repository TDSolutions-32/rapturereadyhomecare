Privacy policy

How this website handles your information. Last updated September 28, 2026.

What we collect

This website does not collect personal information on its own. When you use our contact or care request forms, your details (such as your name, phone number and email) are placed into an email in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you choose to send that email to Contact@raptureready.care.

If you call, email or send us a message, we use what you share only to respond to you and to arrange care.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you change the text size, your browser remembers that choice on your own device so pages stay easy to read. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Protecting your information

We use appropriate practices to protect the information you share with us against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Please don’t send medical records through email; we’ll discuss health details with you by phone.

Other websites

Our pages link to other websites, such as Onslow County programs, Google Maps and helpful articles. We don’t control those sites and aren’t responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the date at the top of this page.
