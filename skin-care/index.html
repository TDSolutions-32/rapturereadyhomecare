Gentle glow: skincare routines after 65

Simple, effective skincare routines for every skin type — made for Onslow County’s sun and humidity.

Here in Onslow County, where warm weather, sunshine, and humidity are part of daily life, understanding your skin’s needs can make all the difference. Let’s explore easy, effective skincare routines designed especially for older adults.

Understanding Your Skin Type

Before choosing products, it’s helpful to understand your skin type:

  • Dry Skin: Feels tight, flaky, or itchy, especially after washing.
  • Sensitive Skin: Prone to redness, irritation, or stinging.
  • Oily Skin: Appears shiny and may have larger pores.
  • Combination Skin: Dry in some areas (like cheeks) and oily in others (such as the forehead or nose).

Your skin type may change as you age, so it’s worth paying attention to how your skin feels day to day.

Skincare Routine for Dry Skin

Dry skin is common among seniors and can feel uncomfortable if not properly cared for.

Gentle Cleanse:
Use a mild, fragrance-free cleanser once daily. Avoid hot water, which can strip natural oils. Warm water is best.

Moisturize Generously:
Apply a rich, creamy moisturizer immediately after bathing, while skin is still slightly damp. Look for ingredients like ceramides, glycerin, or hyaluronic acid.

Extra Care:
Use a humidifier indoors during colder months and drink plenty of water to stay hydrated from the inside out.

Skincare Routine for Sensitive Skin

Sensitive skin benefits from simplicity and consistency.

Choose Gentle Products:
Select fragrance-free, hypoallergenic cleansers and moisturizers. Avoid products with alcohol, dyes, or strong exfoliants.

Patch Test:
Before trying a new product, test it on a small area of skin to make sure it doesn’t cause irritation.

Sun Protection:
Use a gentle mineral sunscreen with zinc oxide or titanium dioxide to protect sensitive skin from sun damage.

Skincare Routine for Oily Skin

Even in later years, some people continue to experience oily skin.

Light Cleansing:
Use a gentle foaming or gel cleanser to remove excess oil without overdrying.

Oil-Free Moisturizer:
Yes—oily skin still needs moisture! Choose a lightweight, non-comedogenic moisturizer to keep skin balanced.

Blot, Don’t Scrub:
If shine appears during the day, gently blot with a tissue rather than washing repeatedly.

Skincare Routine for Combination Skin

Combination skin needs balance and flexibility.

Targeted Care:
Use a gentle cleanser and apply lighter moisturizer on oily areas and richer cream on dry areas.

Adjust as Needed:
Your skin may change with the seasons, so be willing to modify your routine throughout the year.

Daily Sun Protection Is Essential

No matter your skin type, sunscreen is one of the most important steps in your routine.

Choose a broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher and apply it daily, even on cloudy days. Sun protection helps prevent premature aging, sunspots, and skin cancer.

Wearing wide-brimmed hats, sunglasses, and lightweight long sleeves can also offer added protection while enjoying the beautiful outdoors of Onslow County.

Simple Self-Care for Healthy Skin

Healthy skin is supported by healthy habits. Eating balanced meals, drinking water, getting enough sleep, and managing stress all play a role in keeping your skin looking and feeling its best.

If you notice new rashes, persistent itching, sores that won’t heal, or unusual changes in your skin, be sure to talk with your healthcare provider.

Embrace Your Skin, Every Step of the Way

Your skin tells the story of a life well lived. With a gentle skincare routine and a little daily care, you can stay comfortable, confident, and radiant at any age.

Remember—simple steps, taken consistently, can make a beautiful difference.

Your skin deserves kindness, care, and a little extra love. 💛
