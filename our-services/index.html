Our services

Quality senior and adult home care and companionship from compassionate, trained caregivers — from a few hours a week to around-the-clock support.

Choose the level of care

Our in-home aides and companions help adults, seniors and elderly loved ones with daily living, in the comfort of their own home. Their safety and wellness is our top priority.

  • Hourly care

    A few hours, when it helps most

    Hourly care offers you the flexibility to schedule visits when your loved one needs the most help — mornings, meals, bath time or a standing weekly visit.

  • Overnight care

    A 12-hour visit through the night

    A caregiver stays through the night for a twelve-hour visit, so your loved one is safe and you can rest.

  • 24/7 care

    Live-in, around the clock

    Live-in caregivers stay in the home 24 hours a day and assist your loved one at all hours.

  • Specialty care

    Dementia, Alzheimer’s and rehabilitation

    Our caregivers can assist your loved one with special needs, including dementia, Alzheimer’s or rehabilitation after an illness, injury or surgery.

Common questions

What areas do you serve?

We care for seniors and adults in Jacksonville, NC and surrounding communities in Eastern North Carolina. Call us to confirm your address is in our service area.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We accept most major insurances, and private pay is welcome too. Call 1 (252) 503-2978 and we’ll help you check your plan. Our NPI for insurance use is 1720889108.

Can a caregiver help someone living with dementia?

Yes. Our specialty care supports loved ones with dementia and Alzheimer’s, as well as people in rehabilitation after an illness, injury or surgery.

How long is an overnight visit?

Overnight care is a twelve-hour visit. If your loved one needs someone at all hours, ask about 24/7 live-in care.

Can you help with rides to appointments?

Transfer assistance and non-emergency medical transportation are provided through our sister company, Faithful Rides.

What happens if we’re not happy with the care?

Tell us. We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee and will do everything we can to make it right.

Talk with us about care

Tell us a little about your loved one. We’ll talk through schedules, insurance or private pay, and what help at home could look like.
