Care at home, with dignity

Rapture Ready Home Care provides quality in-home care to seniors and adults in and around Jacksonville, NC, so they can live happier, healthier and fuller lives in the comfort of their own home.

Why we do this work

We believe every adult deserves the right to live in the comfort of their own home with dignity.

That’s why we aim to give every family peace of mind — knowing we’ll treat their loved one with the care and respect they deserve. When you hire people to care for someone you love, you want people you can trust and who provide top-rate care. That is exactly our specialty.

Our in-home care balances independence, dignity and quality of life while keeping your loved one safe. Rapture Ready caregivers receive thorough training, ongoing support and the resources they need to deliver a care experience you can count on.

Hearts that serve

“Where compassion meets calling — and care becomes a ministry of excellence.”

At Rapture Ready Home Care, every act of service is guided by both heart and purpose. Our caregivers don’t just provide assistance; they answer a calling to serve with grace, skill and unwavering dedication. Compassion fuels our care — professionalism defines our standard.

Why families choose us

  • Screened, trained caregivers

    Every caregiver is carefully selected, background checked and supervised by licensed staff.

  • Insurance or private pay

    We accept most major insurances, which keeps out-of-pocket costs down. Call and we’ll check your plan.

  • Flexible scheduling

    However much or little help is needed — different times, different days — we build the schedule around your family.

  • 100% satisfaction guarantee

    If you are ever unhappy with the service you receive, we will do everything we can to make it right.

Let us treat your loved ones like family

You can’t always be there. Our dedicated caregivers are ready to make your life easier by providing quality care and assistance for your loved one at home.

And when a trip to the doctor is part of the day, our sister company Faithful Rides can provide the transportation.

Talk with us about care

Tell us a little about your loved one. We’ll talk through schedules, insurance or private pay, and what help at home could look like.
