You can’t always be there. But we can.

Rapture Ready Home Care sends trained, screened caregivers into your loved one’s home — for a few hours, overnight or around the clock — so they can live well, safely, where they’re most comfortable.

Care that fits the hours you need

From a morning visit to a caregiver who lives in, choose the level of help that fits your loved one today — and change it as needs change.

  • Hourly care

    A few hours, when it helps most

    Hourly care offers you the flexibility to schedule visits when your loved one needs the most help — mornings, meals, bath time or a standing weekly visit.

  • Overnight care

    A 12-hour visit through the night

    A caregiver stays through the night for a twelve-hour visit, so your loved one is safe and you can rest.

  • 24/7 care

    Live-in, around the clock

    Live-in caregivers stay in the home 24 hours a day and assist your loved one at all hours.

  • Specialty care

    Dementia, Alzheimer’s and rehabilitation

    Our caregivers can assist your loved one with special needs, including dementia, Alzheimer’s or rehabilitation after an illness, injury or surgery.

What our caregivers help with

Everyday help that keeps your loved one safe, clean, fed and connected — with the right balance of independence, dignity and quality of life.
